Food: eat known cultivated food or packaged rations before foraging. Do not taste unknown plants, mushrooms, or unfamiliar meat. Herbology notes in this repo are cautions, not a menu. Hunger is not a reason to experiment.

Pack food you already eat, with the first night's portion on top of the bag. Eat small amounts through the day. A pot and a spoon you have used at home beat a new wild food. This sets no calorie target and no meal plan for a clinic.

Cook known food until it is hot through, and keep raw scraps away from the cooked pot. Seal or hang leftovers so animals are not invited to where you sleep. A bulging can or a sour smell means you discard it. Do not taste food to decide if it has turned.

Berries, fish, and greens are a local-rules question, and only for kinds you already know from a source outside this note. If you are not sure, it does not go in your mouth. Mushrooms are not a trial. Cultivated food is the plan. Wild food is not a backup menu and not a dose guide.
