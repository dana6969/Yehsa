CHAPTER 1
EQUIPMENT
A pressure canner, a jar lifter, clean jars, new lids and a timer are the basic equipment for home canning.

CHAPTER 2
JARS AND LIDS
Use jars made for canning. Check every rim for chips, and use new flat lids each time.

CHAPTER 3
PRESSURE CANNING MEAT
Section 1. Preparing the meat. Trim fat and cut the meat into pieces that fit the jars with one inch of headspace.
Section 2. Processing. Process pints for 75 minutes and quarts for 90 minutes at the pressure your altitude calls for.
Section 3. Cooling. Let the canner drop to zero pressure on its own before opening it.

CHAPTER 4
STORAGE
Store sealed jars in a cool, dark place and use them within a year. As chapter 3 says, the second section covers processing times.
