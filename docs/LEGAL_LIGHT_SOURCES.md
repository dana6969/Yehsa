# Legal light sources

Legal light shows shooting hours for deer, only where the current official rule was read and cited. Read on 2026-11-17 for the 2026-27 season. Any other state or species shows the common half-hour rule with a plain warning to check the state regulations. Yehsa does not know season dates or units.

When no GPS fix is held the sun times use the middle of the state, so they can differ by a few minutes. The rule itself is the cited one.

| State | Rule used | Sunday | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ME | 30 min before sunrise to 30 min after sunset | No hunting | Maine Dept. of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife, Legal Hunting Hours, and the 2026-27 Maine Hunting quick reference guide. https://www1.maine.gov/ifw/hunting-trapping/hunting/laws-rules/legal-hunting-hours.html |
| NH | 30 min before sunrise to 30 min after sunset | Not decided by this rule | New Hampshire RSA 207:3, I, Lawful Methods of Taking (wildlife is taken between 1/2 hour before sunrise and 1/2 hour after sunset). https://gc.nh.gov/RSA/html/XVIII/207/207-3.htm |
| VT | 30 min before sunrise to 30 min after sunset | Not decided by this rule | Vermont 10 V.S.A. 4745, Taking big game out of season prohibited; time (between one-half hour before sunrise and one-half hour after sunset). https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/section/10/113/04745 |
| MA | 30 min before sunrise to 30 min after sunset | No hunting | MassWildlife, Deer hunting regulations (Hunting hours begin 1/2 hour before sunrise and end 1/2 hour after sunset; hunting is prohibited on Sundays). https://www.mass.gov/info-details/deer-hunting-regulations |
| RI | 30 min before sunrise to 30 min after sunset | Not decided by this rule | Rhode Island 250-RICR-60-00-9, section 9.7 Deer, F (legal shooting hours for hunting deer are one half hour before sunrise to one half hour after sunset). https://rules.sos.ri.gov/Regulations/part/250-60-00-9?reg_id=13477 |
| CT | 30 min before sunrise to sunset | Not decided by this rule | Connecticut DEEP, 2026 Connecticut Hunting and Trapping Guide, Deer Hunting (hunting hours: 1/2 hour before sunrise until sunset). https://portal.ct.gov/deep/hunting/2026-connecticut-hunting-and-trapping-guide/deer-hunting |
| NY | 30 min before sunrise to 30 min after sunset | Not decided by this rule | NYSDEC, 2026-27 New York Hunting and Trapping Guide (big game hunting hours are 1/2-hour before sunrise to 1/2-hour after sunset). https://dec.ny.gov/sites/default/files/2024-07/htgbiggame.pdf |
| PA | 30 min before sunrise to 30 min after sunset | Not decided by this rule | Pennsylvania Game Commission, 2026-27 Pocket Guide (legal hunting hours for all small and big game are one-half hour before sunrise to one-half hour after sunset). https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/pgc/documents/hunttrap/law/26-27%20pocket%20guide.pdf |

Notes carried on screen:

- ME: Maine uses Bangor sunrise and sunset for the whole state.
- ME: Sunday hunting is illegal in Maine.
- ME: Migratory birds, coyote and raccoon have their own hours.
- NH: New Hampshire Fish and Game publishes a sunrise and sunset table. Use it when the minute matters.
- VT: Vermont Fish and Wildlife publishes shooting hours tables. Use them when the minute matters.
- MA: Hunting is prohibited on Sundays in Massachusetts.
- MA: MassWildlife links a sunrise and sunset table from the same page.
- RI: Sunday hunting rules in Rhode Island depend on the town and the land. Check with DEM Fish and Wildlife.
- CT: Connecticut deer hours end at sunset, not half an hour after.
- CT: Sunday deer hunting is private land only, and not within 40 yards of blazed public trails.
- NY: NYSDEC publishes a sunrise and sunset table for the minute. Sunday hunting is allowed in all areas of New York.
- PA: Pennsylvania seasons include Sundays, except migratory game birds. The Game Commission digest has the hours tables and the meridian map.
